# Hydraulics Direct batch 8 — `hd-flareless-tube-fittings`

**30 listings, 268 part numbers** of inch flareless bite-type tube fittings (SAE J514) in forged,
trivalent-zinc-plated carbon steel, unbranded, `IH-ST-<supplier part>`, in a new
`flareless-bite-type-tube-fittings` category under Hydraulic Adapters (position 24).

Connectors to NPTF, ORB and JIC; unions (incl. large hex and bulkhead), elbows, tees, cross;
nuts, sleeves, caps, plugs, locknut, stand-tube reducer. Size labels from the part number. No
pressure ratings published by the source; pages say the rating depends on the tube wall.
Images: studio photos where present; `47525` has none (its photo shows a straight bulkhead union).
