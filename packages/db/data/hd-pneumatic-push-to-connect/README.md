# Hydraulics Direct batch 9 — `hd-pneumatic-push-to-connect`

**18 listings, 81 part numbers** of inch push-to-connect (one-touch) pneumatic fittings for
nylon/PU air tube 5/32"–1/2", unbranded, `IH-PN-<supplier part without the PC- prefix>`, in a
new `push-to-connect-pneumatic-fittings` category under Hydraulic Adapters (position 25).

NPT male/female connectors and elbows, banjo, run/branch tees, Y, unions (straight, reducing,
elbow, tee, cross, bulkhead), plug and tube-end cap. Rating as published by the source:
0–150 psi, vacuum to −29.5 inHg, 32–140 °F, compressed air only. The source copy calls every
body nickel-plated brass; the photos show plastic bodies on the tube-to-tube parts and plastic
bodies with brass threads on the elbows and tees, so the Material spec follows the photos.
Port labels come from the supplier's variant names; `(run)`/`(branch)` marks tee ports.
