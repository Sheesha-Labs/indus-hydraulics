# Hydraulics Direct batch 14 — `hd-stainless-tubing`

**4 listings, 105 part numbers** of inch-O.D. stainless instrumentation tubing, unbranded
`IH-TB-*`, in a new `stainless-steel-instrumentation-tubing` category under Instrumentation
Tube Fittings (position 3): 304 and 316 seamless (ASTM A269/A213) and welded
(ASTM A269/A270, 3-A). 20 ft lengths, 200 ft minimum. No pressure ratings published.

Source fixes: walls `0.350` and `0.650` are 0.035" and 0.065" (impossible on 3/16" and 1/2"
tube; part numbers carry the corrected wall); `ST4-20-0.065` is named 0.035" and listed as
0.065" per its code; the seamless codes' `-200-feet` suffix is dropped.
