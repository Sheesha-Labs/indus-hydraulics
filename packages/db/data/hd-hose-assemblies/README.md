# Hydraulics Direct batch 6 — `hd-hose-assemblies`

**8 listings, 501 part numbers**: the source's 34 hose-assembly families merged by end
configuration (one listing per configuration, every size and length a row), under a new
`hydraulic-hose-assemblies` category in Hoses & Fittings. Part numbers `IH-HA-<supplier part>`.

- 7 listings of 2-wire steel braid hose assemblies (1/4" to 1" ID, 12" to 300"): JIC × JIC,
  JIC × NPT, NPT × NPT, NPT × NPT swivel, JIC × JIC 90°, ORFS × ORFS, ORFS × ORFS 90°.
- 1 listing of 3/8" pressure-washer assemblies (4,000 and 6,000 psi, 50–100 ft).
- Hose working pressure per size is the manufacturer's (from its part names); pages say an
  assembly is rated to the lower of hose and ends, and that NPT ends limit the larger sizes
  (SAE J514: 3/4" 3,000 psi, 1" 2,000 psi). `L` is the overall assembly length in mm.
- The source's hose brand name is not used (listed unbranded).
- **No images**: every source photo shows a legible "Titan Ultraflex … PSI WP" layline on the hose.
  Candidates for an edit that removes the layline, or for new photos from the principal.
